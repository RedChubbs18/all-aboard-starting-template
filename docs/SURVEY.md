# Quick colleague survey: what would most improve onboarding?

**Yes, it's worth doing.** It gives the pitch a real, local measure instead of an assumed one, and takes about 30 minutes to set up. Run it as a **Microsoft Form** inside the tenant, so it stays within the event rules, and share it in Teams or with office colleagues who aren't involved in the hackathon. It's anonymous, with no personal data collected. Aim for 15 or more responses.

## Questions (about 2 minutes to complete)

1. **When did you join the group?** (Less than 6 months / 6–24 months / 2–5 years / 5+ years)
2. **How would you rate your onboarding overall?** (1–5 stars)
3. **Which of these caused you the most trouble?** (pick up to 3)
   - Not knowing what was happening between offer and day one
   - Vetting (PEV) delays or unclear steps
   - Laptop or equipment not ready
   - System access (accounts, passwords, Authenticator) not ready
   - Building pass or office access
   - Not knowing who my team or buddy was
   - Booking induction or mandatory training
   - My line manager not having things ready
   - Too many different places and emails to keep track of
   - Other (please say)
4. **Rank these ideas by how much they'd improve onboarding.** (ranking question; these map to MyJourney features)
   - One place showing every step from offer to day 30, with progress
   - Live vetting status with reminders and a contact if it's stuck
   - Choosing my laptop and kit before day one
   - Meeting my team and buddy (with Teams chat) before I start
   - An assistant that answers onboarding questions instantly
   - Booking induction and checking my photo for my pass in advance
   - My line manager getting nudges when their tasks are overdue
   - A clear first day: where, when and who to ask for
5. **On your first day, how ready did you feel?** (1–5)
6. **How many days did it take until you had everything you needed to do your job?** (0–1 / 2–5 / 6–10 / 10+)
7. **Would you use an app like MyJourney if you were joining again?** (Yes / Maybe / No)
8. **Anything else that would have helped?** (free text; ask people not to include personal details)

## How to use the results in the pitch

- **Top pain points (Q3):** "X% of colleagues said Y was their biggest onboarding problem." Map each one to the MyJourney feature that fixes it.
- **Idea ranking (Q4):** lead the demo with the highest-ranked feature.
- **Baseline (Q5, Q6):** "Colleagues took a median of N days to be fully set up." The target: everything ready on Day 0. The MI dashboard shows readiness live.
- **Adoption intent (Q7):** "N% would use it."
